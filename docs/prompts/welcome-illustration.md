# Welcome sheet illustration (image prompt)

Where it goes: the "Welcome to Küşt" sheet on Play, in place of the small K mark,
full width above the title. The sheet colour is `#262320`.

Attach as references: 3–4 bot portraits from `assets/bots/` (e.g. Bahar, Mark,
Maya, Berdi), the app icon `docs/logo/kit/kust-app-icon.svg` (render it to PNG
first), and one screenshot of the puzzle carpet.

## Prompt

Wide 16:9 illustration for a chess app's welcome screen, 1600×900. A cosy scene
in a warm, friendly cartoon style that exactly matches the attached character
portraits: the same soft cel shading, clean bold shapes, warm rim light and
expressive, kind faces. Three or four of the attached characters sit and lean
around a low wooden chess table, mid-game, smiling at each other. One reaches
to move a knight, one laughs, one points at the board as if giving a tip.
Keep each character recognisable: same hair, faces, clothes and colours as in
their portrait.

Under the table lies a Turkmen carpet in deep madder red with ivory, indigo
and saffron göl patterns, drawn in a slightly pixelated, knotted style like the
attached carpet screenshot. The chess pieces are simple, rounded and friendly:
cream white and warm charcoal, flat with a soft top-to-bottom shade.

Colours: a very dark warm brown background (#262320) that fades smoothly to
that colour at every edge, so the image melts into a dark app sheet with no
visible border. Accents only in golden yellow (#FFBB00) and a fresh green
(#3CB77C). Soft warm lamp light from above the table, gentle shadows, no harsh
contrast.

Composition: characters and table in the middle 70% of the width, clear space
at the top, nothing important near the edges (the image is cropped to rounded
corners on phones). Readable at phone size: big shapes, few small details.

## Negative prompt

No text, letters, logos or watermarks. No photorealism, no 3D render look, no
neon, no cold blue light. No extra fingers or deformed hands. No busy
background, no other people, no pieces that look like real-world chess brands.

## Output we need

- PNG or WebP, 1600×900 (16:9), sRGB.
- Edges fade to `#262320` (or a transparent PNG with soft edges).
- Save it as `assets/illustrations/welcome.webp`; I'll wire it in.
