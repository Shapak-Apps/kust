Küşt is a chess app that works entirely on your phone.

## What the app stores

Everything the app keeps stays **on your device**:

- your games, ratings, puzzle progress, lesson stars and achievements;
- your settings (board, pieces, language, sound);
- the name you choose for yourself, and a random guest identifier created on first start;
- ratings of your moves, so a game you analysed opens again without waiting.

## What the app does not do

- It has no account, no sign-in and no sign-up.
- It does not ask for any permission beyond what a normal app has. In particular it does **not** use the internet in the release build, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage outside its own folder.
- It does not contain advertising, analytics or any third-party tracking.
- It does not send your games, your name or anything else to us or to anyone else.

## Your data

Because nothing leaves your device, we cannot see, share or sell your data. You can remove everything by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Children

Küşt collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a later version changes any of the above (for example by adding crash reports), this page is updated first and the app's store listing says so.
