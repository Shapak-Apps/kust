# Google Play listing — English

**Title (≤ 30):** Küşt: Chess, Puzzles, Learn
**Short description (≤ 80):** Play chess against bots, learn from zero, solve puzzles. Works offline.

**Full description (≤ 4000):**

Küşt is a calm, friendly chess app for learning and playing — with no ads, no account and no internet needed.

**Learn from the first move.** A guided path takes you from how the pawn moves to your first checkmates, with a coach, hints and stars for every lesson.

**Play bots with their own personality.** Pick an opponent from beginner to master, play rated games to earn your own rating, or switch on practice mode: every move is rated live, you can take moves back, and "Show best" points out the strongest move. Play with a clock (3+2, 5 or 10 minutes) or without.

**Review every game.** See your accuracy, who was winning when, and the moves worth learning from. Step through the game with the best move shown as an arrow.

**Puzzles.** A map of puzzle levels from mate in one to harder ideas, plus a new puzzle every day.

**Pass and play.** Two people, one phone, with a clock if you like.

**Made for your phone.** Dark, easy on the eyes, large pieces, three board styles, smooth animations, English, Russian and Turkmen.

**Private by design.** Your games and progress stay on your device.

Continue an unfinished game any time — Küşt remembers where you stopped.
